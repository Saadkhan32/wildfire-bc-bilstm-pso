# `data/cross_border_US/` — BC ↔ U.S. cross-border comparison (Section 3.10)

Inputs and outputs for `code/cross_border_comparison.py`, which checks whether the BC susceptibility
surface agrees in rank with an independent U.S. model along the 49th parallel (Fig. 19, Table 8).

| File(s) | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `BP_WA_ID_MT.tif` (+ `.tfw`, `.tif.ovr`, `.tif.aux.xml`, `.tif.xml`) | U.S. FSim **annual burn probability** (WA, ID, MT; native 270 m; reprojected to EPSG:3005 in analysis) | USDA Forest Service — Wildfire Risk to Communities |
| `bc_strip.*` | BC sampling strip along 49°N (EPSG:3005) | derived in this study |
| `us_strip.*` | U.S. sampling strip along 49°N (EPSG:3005) | derived in this study |
| `bc_us_border.*` | BC–U.S. border line (EPSG:3005) | derived from boundary data |
| `Fig19_BC_US_percentile_rank.tif` | **Fig. 19a raster** — within-strip percentile rank 0–100 (BC BiLSTM-PSO susceptibility north of 49°N + U.S. FSim burn probability south); EPSG:3005, 1.5 km, nodata −9999 | output of `cross_border_comparison.py` |
| `Fig19b_tile_pairs.csv` | **Fig. 19b data** — per 20 km straddle-tile mean BC susceptibility vs mean U.S. burn probability (n = 34) | output of `cross_border_comparison.py` |
| `Fig19c_Table8_crossborder_stats.json` | **Fig. 19c + Table 8 data** — Spearman ρ across 15/20/25/30 km tiles, quadratic-weighted κ, and near-vs-interior edge tests | output of `cross_border_comparison.py` |

Units differ (BC 0–1 index vs FSim burn probability), so the comparison is by **rank** within straddle-tiles.
The three `Fig19*` files are regenerated by:
`python code/cross_border_comparison.py --bc-susc data/susceptibility/BC_susceptibility_BiLSTM_PSO.tif`
